# Pictures & assets links

Central list of image/asset URLs used in the project.  
Pictures are stored on Cloudinary under the account **jeexpert.service@gmail.com**.

**In code**, use the central registry **`lib/pictures.ts`**: call `getPictureUrl(PICTURE_LABELS.Logo)` or `getPictureUrl(PICTURE_LABELS.LogoApp)` instead of pasting URLs. Add new labels and URLs in `lib/pictures.ts` when you add assets.
